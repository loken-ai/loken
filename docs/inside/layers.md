cards are filled in rank order until every layer has a card.</li><li>When the model keeps a KV cache and every card has a measured speed, the same layers are then shared out by that speed, within what each card holds.</li><li>The output head stays on the card of the last layer, and each layer keeps its KV cache on its own card.</li><li>A token climbs the run of card A, crosses once to card B and advised to stay in the page cache.</li><li>Those layers keep their KV cache in host memory.</li><li>A token climbs the runs of the cards, then crosses to the host and climbs the host run.</li><li>The data-scene="spill"><h3>A mixture whose experts leave the cards</h3><ol class="steps"><li>Every layer's attention, router, shared expert and KV cache go to a card.</li><li>When the experts do not fit with the whole reserve and the card cache serves the file's expert format, the routed experts of every layer stay in the file's mapping.</li><li>Each card keeps a cache of expert slots for its layers.</li><li>With at least 2 cards, a measured read rate and link for each, and room on the cards for every expert, each layer takes its experts back, split across cards by id range where that is predicted faster.</li><li>When the card cache cannot serve the file's expert format, only the experts of the last layers stay in host memory.</li></ol></section><section data-scene="split"><h3>Every layer split across 2 cards</h3><ol class="steps"><li>The model does not fit 1 card, and the split is predicted faster for 1 token and for a prompt chunk, or the layers would otherwise reach the host.</li><li>Every layer is split across the 2 cards, each card's share following its read rate.</li><li>Each card holds its share of the heads and the feed-forward, or a range of a mixture's experts with the router whole on every card.</li><li>Each card computes its share of the attention heads, then the 2 partial outputs are summed onto both cards.</li><li>Each card computes its share of the feed-forward, then the outputs are summed again.</li><li>A layer that cannot be split runs whole on the first card.</li><li>After the last layer, the output head on the first card gives the logits.</li></ol><p>Steps 4 and 5 repeat for every layer.</p><p>The sums cross the link between the 2 cards twice per layer. Across machines, the cluster cuts the stack into runs of layers instead.</p></section><section data-scene="ladder"><h3>The placement ladder</h3><ol class="steps"><li>The planned placement fails on the cards with an out-of-memory error.</li><li>Rung 2, <code>an even split over 2 cards</code>, shares the layers out by each card's compute speed, whatever its free memory.</li><li>Rung 3, <code>the cards at 70% with the host</code>, then at 50%, 35% and 20%, offers that share of each card's free memory and leaves the rest off the cards.</li><li>Rung 4, <code>the host</code>, puts every layer off the cards.</li><li>A failure that is not out of memory goes straight to the host rung, and a rung that repeats a tried placement is skipped.</li><li>At request time, an out-of-memory error hands the pools back, evicts idle models one by one, then reloads the model with a larger reserve on the cards.</li><li>An idle model moves whole to the fastest card it does not use that has room, and a model already on 1 card moves only to a faster card.</li></ol></section></div><p class="sr" id="ana-layers-sr" aria-live="polite"></p>
